## Nocci 0.3.1

A small update that makes Nocci's wording clearer and more consistent. Nothing about how
it works has changed, and your settings stay as they are.

- **Claude Code setup reads correctly in every state.** If your status line was removed
  after setup, Nocci now says so plainly, and its buttons say exactly what they will do.
  A setup that is already complete no longer points at a button you cannot press.
- **Sessions:** reinstalling the hooks after moving Nocci now says Reinstall throughout.
- **Settings** describe what each option does more precisely, including the small mark
  above each bar that Pace adds.
- **Menus and messages** use one consistent style.

Requires macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon.
